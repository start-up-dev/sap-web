# ShipSafe Homepage Strategy
> The homepage has one job: make the right visitor self-select into the right persona page — while converting the ones who are ready to buy right now.

---

## The Homepage's Role in the Funnel

Your three persona pages (`/for-vibe-coders`, `/for-saas-founders`, `/for-web-agencies`) are conversion machines built for one specific person with one specific problem. They're long, emotional, and specific.

The homepage is different. It has to do three things simultaneously:

1. **Validate** that ShipSafe is for people like me (fast qualifier)
2. **Convert** visitors who already know what they want (direct buyers)
3. **Route** curious visitors toward the page that fits them (segmentation)

Think of the homepage as your storefront window. The persona pages are the rooms inside.

---

## Homepage Architecture

### Section 1: Hero — The Universal Fear
Every one of your customers shares one underlying emotion: **"I don't actually know if my app is secure."**

The hero doesn't need to speak to one persona. It speaks to that universal feeling.

**Headline:**
> *"You built it. You shipped it. Do you actually know if it's secure?"*

**Subline:**
> *"ShipSafe runs 38+ active security checks on any app or website in minutes — and gives you a plain-English report you can act on, or share with the enterprise client asking for one. Free quick scan. Full audit for $29."*

**CTA — two options:**
- Primary: `Scan My App Free`
- Secondary: `See What's Included`

**Why this works:** It's broad enough to capture all three personas — the anxious builder, the deal-blocker founder, and the agency owner all feel "I don't actually know if my app is secure." The subline then plants the enterprise angle (`share with the enterprise client`) and the agency angle (`any app or website`) without committing to either.

---

### Section 2: The Three Paths (Persona Routing)
This is the most important section on your homepage. After the hero, you need to help visitors self-select.

**Section label:** `Who is ShipSafe for?`

Three cards, side by side:

**Card 1 — The Builder**
- Icon: code/rocket
- Headline: *"You built with AI tools and want to know if it's actually safe"*
- Body: *"Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, v0. You moved fast. Make sure nothing's broken."*
- CTA: `I'm a builder →` → `/for-vibe-coders`

**Card 2 — The Founder**
- Icon: deal/handshake
- Headline: *"An enterprise prospect just asked if you've been pen-tested"*
- Body: *"Get a professional audit report in minutes — not weeks. Close the deal."*
- CTA: `I'm closing a deal →` → `/for-saas-founders`

**Card 3 — The Agency**
- Icon: agency/building
- Headline: *"You build sites for clients and want to protect your reputation"*
- Body: *"Audit before every handover. Add it as a premium service. Sleep at night."*
- CTA: `I run an agency →` → `/for-web-agencies`

**Why this works:** Self-segmentation is more powerful than trying to write copy for everyone. When someone sees their exact situation described in two sentences, they click. This section turns a generic homepage into a funnel.

---

### Section 3: How It Works (Universal, Brief)
Keep it to three steps. Short and scannable. Everyone needs to understand the mechanism before they trust the product.

1. **Submit your URL** — Any URL. No setup. No verification.
2. **We run 38+ checks** — Active testing. Not a surface scan. OWASP Top 10 and more.
3. **You get a real report** — Plain English. Severity ratings. Fix steps. PDF export.

---

### Section 4: The Number That Earns Trust
One bold stat section. Make it visual.

- **38+** security checks per audit
- **5 min** average scan time
- **$29** vs $10,000 for a traditional pentest
- **100%** of findings include a fix — not just a problem

---

### Section 5: Social Proof (One from Each Persona)
Pull one testimonial from each persona page — so every visitor type sees themselves reflected.

- Alex (vibe coder): *"Found two findings in 5 minutes that would have been really bad."*
- Sarah (SaaS founder): *"Ran ShipSafe, sent the PDF that afternoon, closed the contract two days later."*
- Marcus (agency): *"Run it before every client launch now. Caught a serious misconfiguration. Client never knew."*

---

### Section 6: Pricing (Simplified)
Homepage pricing should be minimal. Two plans, clear prices. No feature walls. Send anyone who wants detail to the persona pages.

**Title:** *"Simple. Transparent. No surprises."*

- **Free:** Quick Scan — see what's exposed
- **$29:** Deep Audit — the full report, shareable PDF, every finding explained

Single CTA: `Get My Free Scan`

---

### Section 7: Final CTA — The Decision Moment
The last thing on the page before the footer. This is for people who scrolled the whole page and are ready.

**Headline:** *"Find what's broken. Fix it. Move forward."*
**Subline:** *"Free scan takes 5 minutes. You'll know exactly where you stand."*
**CTA:** `Scan My App Free — No Card Required`

## SEO Strategy for the Homepage

The homepage should own the high-volume, broad-intent keywords. The persona pages own the specific, high-conversion keywords.

**Homepage target keywords:**
- `website security audit` (high volume, broad)
- `security audit tool` (broad)
- `pentest alternative` (comparison intent)
- `app security check` (broad)
- `free security scan` (high volume, top of funnel)

**Meta title:** `ShipSafe — Security Audit for Apps & Websites | Free Scan`

**Meta description:** `Run 38+ active security checks on any app or website in minutes. Get a plain-English report with fix steps. Free quick scan. Full audit for $29. No pentest required.`

---

## What NOT to Put on the Homepage

- **Long feature lists** — Save those for persona pages. The homepage's job is routing and trust, not education.
- **Technical jargon** — OWASP, ZAP, Nikto are reassuring on persona pages once trust is established. On the homepage they create friction.
- **Multiple CTAs fighting each other** — Every section should have one clear next action. Decision fatigue kills conversions.
- **Pricing complexity** — Two plans, two prices, one CTA. That's it.

```

├── /for-vibe-coders       ← "I built with AI tools"
├── /for-saas-founders     ← "I need a report for an enterprise deal"
```

Every persona page links back to pricing and to the homepage. The homepage links to all three. This creates a clean funnel and strong internal SEO structure.
